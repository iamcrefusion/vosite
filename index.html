Hello and Welcome!

My name is David but people have been calling my Guff for years. Welcome to my site!

I have been absorbed with the power of voice since I first booted up Starcraft in 1998 and listened to some of the most immersing voice acting I had heard at the time.

Voice acting is a passion for me.
The pride of a moving performance is my drive to create memorable moments for the audience.

It is the ability to create a world of characters and stories with just my voice that has me hooked.
I love the challenge of bringing a vision to life.

If you would like to get in touch, please send me an email!

Skills & Services:

  • I work from a broadcast quality studio using a TLM102 plugging into an Audient iD4.
  • I have a naturally deeper range which I enjoy manipulating to various styles
  • Character Voices for Video Games, TV and Animation
  • Commercial Voiceovers for Radio, TV and Online Ads
  • Narration for Audiobooks, Documentaries and E-learning
  • Voice Acting for Dubbing and Localization Projects
  • Directed live sessions via Discord, Source Connect, Zoom, FaceTime
